# Mobile

The Orca Lab app pairs with your desktop and puts your agents in your pocket. iOS builds ship
through TestFlight; Android APKs are attached to the
[`mobile-android-v*` releases](https://github.com/ab2webco/orca-oss/releases?q=mobile-android).

## Pair

Open **Settings > Mobile** on the desktop and scan the QR code from the app. After that the phone
reaches the desktop directly on your network, or through an end-to-end encrypted relay when it
cannot.

## What you can do from the phone

- **Live terminals.** Watch any agent's terminal stream and type into it.
- **Owner-question Inbox.** When a lead needs a decision only you can make, the question lands in
  the Inbox with a notification. Answer it there and the answer goes back to the lead.
- **Notification tap routing.** A tap opens the right host and screen; a lead's question opens the
  lead's terminal with the Team drawer.
- **Team drawer.** Over the lead's terminal: open questions, a card per member with its ticket,
  state, PR and CI, and the team's merges per day and median lead time.
- **Plane board and plugin panels**, and a button that asks the desktop to install its pending
  update.

The lead and its members are described in the [Orca Game reference](../reference/orchestrator-game.md).
